# QRDine Master SaaS Agreement

**Version:** 2026.1  
**Governing Law:** Laws of the Republic of India  
**Jurisdiction:** Courts of New Delhi, India  
**Statutory Classification:** Software Technology Intermediary under Section 79, Information Technology Act, 2000

---

## 1. Parties & Preamble

This Master Software-as-a-Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between:
1. **QRDine Technologies Private Limited** ("QRDine", "Company", "Service Provider"), a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office in New Delhi, India; and
2. **The Restaurant Entity** ("Restaurant", "Client", "Data Fiduciary") as identified in the onboarding declaration or subscription profile.

---

## 2. Statutory Legal Demarcation & Intermediary Demarcation (Section 79 IT Act)

2.1. **Intermediary Status:** QRDine operates strictly as a **Technology Intermediary** as defined under Section 2(1)(w) of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and is entitled to safe harbour immunity under **Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000** and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.

2.2. **No Merchant of Record:** QRDine is not an online food delivery aggregator, not a payment aggregator, and not a merchant of record. QRDine does not buy, sell, prepare, inspect, package, handle, or transport food items.

2.3. **Independent Merchant Account (BYO-PG):** All diner payments flow directly from diner payment instruments to the Restaurant’s own registered payment gateway merchant account (Razorpay, Cashfree, Paytm, etc.) or counter POS cash desk. QRDine never holds diner settlement funds.

---

## 3. Allocation of Responsibilities

### 3.1. Restaurant Obligations & Sole Liabilities
The Restaurant explicitly covenants, warrants, and agrees that it is solely liable and responsible for:
1. **Food Quality, Safety & Hygiene:** Ensuring full compliance with the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (FSSA) and FSSAI regulations.
2. **Allergen Declarations & Dietary Accuracy:** Truthfully and accurately classifying items as Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, or Containing Egg, and identifying known allergens (nuts, gluten, milk, soy, shellfish) in menu catalog setups.
3. **Menu Pricing & Statutory Display:** Complying with Legal Metrology Act rules regarding price display, Maximum Retail Price (MRP), and restaurant menu pricing.
4. **GST Collection & Statutory Remittance:** Accurate classification of supplies (5% Composition Scheme under Section 10 CGST Act without ITC vs 18% Regular GST with ITC), timely filing of GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, and generation of customer tax invoices.
5. **Physical Order Fulfillment:** Timely preparation, table delivery, packaging, and service of customer orders placed via QR codes.

### 3.2. QRDine Obligations
QRDine covenants and agrees that it is solely responsible for:
1. **Platform Availability:** Complying with targeted 99.9% uptime for the cloud digital menu and order management software.
2. **Data Security & Technical Infrastructure:** Maintaining enterprise-grade encryption (AES-256-GCM), secure database isolation (`cafeId` multi-tenant keys), and regular automated backup snapshots.
3. **Log Integrity & Regulatory Directives:** Retaining access and authentication audit trails for a minimum of 180 days in compliance with CERT-In directions and synchronizing server clocks with Indian Standard Time (NPL/NIC NTP servers).

## 4. Financial & Invoicing Terms (SAC 998313)

4.1. **Subscription Billing:** QRDine bills the Restaurant for SaaS software subscriptions under Service Accounting Code **SAC 998313** (*Information technology design and development services / Software cloud hosting*).

4.2. **Statutory 18% GST:** All platform subscription fees are subject to 18% Goods and Services Tax:
- **Intra-State Supply (Delhi to Delhi):** 9% CGST + 9% SGST.
- **Inter-State Supply (Delhi to other Indian States/UTs):** 18% IGST.

4.3. **0% Order Commission:** QRDine does not charge per-order percentage commissions on diner food sales.

---

## 5. Term, Termination & Post-Termination Data Retention

5.1. **Right to Terminate:** Either party may terminate for convenience upon 30 days' written notice, or immediately in the event of a material breach or critical security compromise.

5.2. **Statutory Accounting Retention:** In compliance with Section 36 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, invoice books, sales registers, and ledger manifests are retained in immutable encrypted storage for a statutory period of **8 years (72 months)** following the relevant financial year.

---

## 6. Indemnification & Dispute Resolution

6.1. **Restaurant Indemnity:** The Restaurant agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless QRDine, its directors, and employees against any consumer claims, FSSAI regulatory penalties, food adulteration actions, or tax liabilities arising from the Restaurant's food operations.

6.2. **Arbitration:** Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration in New Delhi under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 by a sole arbitrator appointed mutually by the parties.
